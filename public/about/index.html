ABOUT US

Driven by Ideas. Powered by Technology.

ZAQSA is a premier technology firm based in New Delhi, specializing in high-performance web development, mobile applications, AI workflow automation, and custom enterprise software for ambitious companies.

WHO WE ARE

Founded in New Delhi, ZAQSA Tech and Solutions Private Limited was established with a singular conviction: technology should accelerate business velocity, not complicate it. We saw ambitious businesses struggling with disjointed off-the-shelf software, brittle legacy systems, and slow development cycles that drained momentum.

ZAQSA bridges the gap between deep technical engineering and real-world business outcomes. Our multidisciplinary team combines full-stack software architecture, mobile engineering, and modern artificial intelligence to build software that is robust, beautiful, and built for scale.

Today, we partner with startups, growing enterprises, and established institutions across India and internationally, delivering mission-critical applications, automated operations, and modern digital experiences that give our clients an unfair competitive advantage.
